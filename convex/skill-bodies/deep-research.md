# Deep Research

Systematic methodology for thorough web research. The quality of output depends on the quality and quantity of research — never generate content from general knowledge alone.

## Phase 1: Broad exploration

1. Initial survey searches to understand the overall landscape
2. Identify key subtopics, dimensions, and angles from the results
3. Map different perspectives and stakeholders

## Phase 2: Deep dive

For each important dimension:
1. Targeted searches with precise keywords, multiple phrasings
2. Read important sources in full, not just snippets
3. Follow references to other important resources

## Phase 3: Diversity & validation

| Information type | Purpose | Example |
|-----------------|---------|---------|
| Facts & data | Concrete evidence | "statistics", "market size" |
| Examples & cases | Real-world application | "case study", "implementation" |
| Expert opinions | Authority | "expert analysis", "interview" |
| Trends | Direction | "trends", "forecast" |
| Comparisons | Context | "vs", "alternatives" |
| Challenges | Balance | "limitations", "criticism" |

## Phase 4: Synthesis check — before writing, verify

- Searched from at least 3-5 different angles?
- Read the most important sources in full?
- Concrete data, examples, AND expert perspectives?
- Both positives and challenges covered?
- Information current and authoritative?

Any NO → keep researching.

## Search tips

- Be specific: "enterprise AI adoption trends 2026" not "AI trends"
- Hint authority: "research paper", "McKinsey report", "industry analysis"
- Always use the ACTUAL current date/year in temporal queries — never a hardcoded past year. "Latest/today" needs month+day precision.

## Quality bar

Research is sufficient when you can answer: key facts? 2-3 concrete examples? expert views? current trends? challenges? why relevant now?

## Common mistakes

- Stopping after 1-2 searches
- Relying on snippets without reading sources
- One angle on a multi-faceted topic
- Ignoring contradictions
- Outdated info when current data exists
- Generating content before research is complete
